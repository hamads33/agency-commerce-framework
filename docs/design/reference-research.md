# Forge reference research

Reviewed the Xinzuo Australia homepage and chef-knife collection in Chromium at 1440px and 390px. Research captures are kept under the local build directory and are excluded from Forge distribution.

The reference pairs a high-contrast close-up with a direct shopping action. The desktop header provides several ways into a deep inventory, while mobile gives search, cart and a drawer priority. Collection pages explain the purpose of the category before exposing sorting and material facets. Product cards repeat a clear name, size or series and price hierarchy. Product information expands from a purchase summary into specifications and supporting editorial content.

Forge applies these principles through a warm paper palette, a split editorial hero, three clearly differentiated collections and concise material descriptions. The demo uses a smaller assortment to make product comparisons simpler. Forge uses original generated photography and fictional product copy. It uses no reference assets or endorsements.

Sources: [Xinzuo Australia](https://xinzuo.com.au/) and [Chef Knives](https://xinzuo.com.au/collections/chef-knives).
