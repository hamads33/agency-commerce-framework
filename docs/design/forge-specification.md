# Forge design specification

Forge presents fictional culinary tools under a warm ivory, ink and bronze palette. The wordmark and editorial headings use a restrained serif. Commerce controls use a legible sans-serif and keep the parent’s keyboard behavior.

The homepage opens with an editorial split, then leads into three collections, an essentials grid, a short materials story and a studio notes invitation. At mobile widths the copy and photography stack and catalogue cards remain compact. Category pages inherit native archive sorting, filters and pagination. Single products prioritize WooCommerce's gallery, selection and purchase form, followed by specifications and related products.

The parent theme supplies the header, product card and native WooCommerce layouts. Forge owns its stylesheet. Demo products, menus, assets, category links and homepage composition live in the explicit setup fixture. This separation lets another brand reuse the child theme and replace every demo asset through WordPress.
