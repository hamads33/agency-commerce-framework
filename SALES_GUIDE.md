# Sales Guide

## Buyer Fit

Good fit:

- agencies building multiple WooCommerce stores;
- merchants who need custom presentation and WooCommerce ownership;
- teams that value maintainability over page-builder speed;
- stores that can use WooCommerce-native cart and checkout behavior.

Poor fit:

- teams that need a hosted SaaS platform;
- projects requiring custom payment, tax, shipping, subscription, marketplace,
  or ERP features out of the box;
- non-technical teams expecting drag-and-drop layout control for every surface.

## Discovery Questions

- How many stores or redesigns will reuse the same foundation?
- What checkout customizations are legally or operationally required?
- Who owns long-term updates after launch?
- Are current performance issues caused by heavy builders or duplicated custom
  code?
- Which surfaces need child-theme design control versus plugin functionality?

## Proof Points

- Parent theme, plugin, and child theme are separated.
- Showcase storefronts demonstrate visual range without framework changes.
- Playwright covers storefront visuals across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Packaging excludes development dependencies and test artifacts.
- Block checkout remains WooCommerce-owned.

## Claims To Avoid

Do not claim guaranteed conversion lift, universal Lighthouse scores, automatic
SEO ranking improvement, one-click migration from all platforms, or compatibility
with every WooCommerce extension without project validation.
